A Bird's Eye View of Campsite Availability

Continuously scrapes nearby campgrounds for campsite-level availability, and displays them on a filterable map. Great for getting an idea of which campsites are available across many different campgrounds. Also great for nabbing a spot right on the water or accommodating your friends who have pets.
